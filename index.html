Drawing tools

Tap the pencil (✏️) button over the chart to open the tool strip.

OB / FVG: tap two opposite corners to draw a zone box.
BOS / CHoCH: tap two points to draw a labeled break line.
EQH/EQL: tap two points at the same swing level for a liquidity marker.
H-Line: one tap sets a price level across the whole chart.
Trend: tap two points for a free trendline.
Text: one tap, then type a note.
✋ Edit: drag a drawing, its corners, or its endpoints to adjust it later. Entry/SL/TP lines can also be dragged directly up/down.
🗑 Delete: tap an existing drawing to remove it.

Drawings are anchored to exact time+price, so they stay correctly placed while you smooth-zoom, side-scroll/pan, or replay.
